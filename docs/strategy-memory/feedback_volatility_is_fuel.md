**Concept (LRLR):**
When price breaks into a zone where there are few opposing orders (thin liquidity), it runs quickly toward the next concentration of stops/limit orders. News events are catalysts that trigger these runs. A clean setup positioned BEFORE or just INTO a LRLR gets pulled to target with minimal drawdown.

**Implications for Recon Live:**
1. **Red folder news DO pause** monitor (5 min before, 5 min after) per user directive 2026-04-23. Orange does NOT pause.
2. **Post-news CHoCHs are high-quality** — news flush → reversal CHoCH often has strong delta and tests real liquidity. Treat these as prime S1/S2 candidates.
3. **Wide intraday ranges from overnight news are opportunity, not noise**. The liquidity pools on either side are magnets once one is taken.
4. **Fast fills are good.** A trade that hits T1 in 3 minutes instead of 30 is a LRLR success, not a sign of randomness.

**How to apply:**
- Red news: pause window 5-min-before to 5-min-after ONLY. Outside that window, embrace volatility.
- When evaluating a setup, consider whether it is riding a LRLR (sweep of a big liquidity pool followed by CHoCH): these are the highest-edge scenarios.
- Target selection can be more aggressive toward the NEXT intact liquidity pool beyond current structure, since LRLR moves tend to travel far in one leg.
